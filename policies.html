What we collect on this site

This site is a lookbook and product catalog. It has no accounts and no checkout. The only personal information we collect here is the email address you choose to give us in the “Get Drop 01 first” sign-up. We use it only to email you about Solo Sees drops and launch codes, we store it with our website host (Netlify), and you can unsubscribe or ask us to delete it at any time. We don't use advertising trackers or sell data to anyone.

Like any website, the servers that host this page and deliver its fonts (Google Fonts) may automatically log technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the time of your visit. That data is handled by those providers under their own privacy policies. Your browser may also store small settings locally on your device; these stay on your device and are not sent to us.

When you buy

When you click "Buy it now" you leave this site and check out on Etsy. Etsy collects your name, shipping address, email and payment details and processes your payment. Payment card details are handled by Etsy and are never shared with us. Etsy's privacy policy applies to everything you do there.

To make and ship your order, we receive your name, shipping address and order details from Etsy. We share those with our print-on-demand production partner, which prints and ships the item, and with the shipping carrier. We use this information only to fulfill your order, provide customer service, handle returns and meet our legal, tax and accounting obligations.

How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as we need them to handle your order and any return, and as long as tax and accounting laws require (generally up to seven years).

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it where we're not required by law to keep it. If your item arrives damaged, misprinted, defective or isn't what you ordered, message us through Etsy within 30 days of delivery with your order number and a clear photo of the problem. We'll send a free replacement or a full refund, your choice, and you won't need to send the item back.
