WHO IS WILL KEENAN?

Will Keenan broke out as Tromeo in Troma's Tromeo & Juliet (1996), the punk-rock, blood-soaked Shakespeare riff that turned him into a cult-movie face overnight. He went back to Troma for Terror Firmer (1999), playing the psychotic Casey while also working behind the camera as a producer and casting director.

Trained in experimental and classical theatre at NYU, Will does his own stunts, and getting hit by cars became his trademark. On Operation Midnight Climax (2002), the guerrilla-shot New York comedy he co-wrote, co-directed and starred in, he took four car hits in one day. Critics compared it to Buster Keaton, and it won the Best Feature Audience Award at the Brooklyn International Film Festival.

Since then he's turned up everywhere from Law & Order to Eternal Sunshine of the Spotless Mind, Amityville Aliens, Johnny X and Chop, and built a career as a digital entertainment executive. Now he's back where he started, making his own movies: co-writing and starring in Zombie Parm and starring in Before His Time with his cousins at Solo Sees Studios.
